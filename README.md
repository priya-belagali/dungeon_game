# Eclipse of the Relic — 3-Level C++ OOP Adventure

Features distinct human-like avatars with facial details and hairstyles, multiple villain types (Demon Monkey, Shadow Wraith, Obsidian Golem, Astral Warden), character speech/thought bubbles, distinct chamber visuals, visible hero and villain combat animations, and a 3-level story where Level 2 recovers the magical Relic and Level 3 reveals the hero's villainous plan to destroy the world under a timer.


v10 Additions: Distinct weapons, obstacles & objectives per level; maze navigation (North/South/East/West) with stamina drain (especially South), dead ends, and level-restarting wrong turns; Web Audio SFX + dramatic canvas visuals.


v11 Additions: Extensive 9-room North/South/East/West maze with weapons & demons across East and West wings as well; physical East/West/North/South character turning and walking animations on canvas.

v12 Additions: Live Maze Minimap drawn directly on the canvas showing all 9 North/South/East/West chambers and current position; fixed East/West exploration & character turning in both WASM and standalone modes with weapons & demons across East and West wings.

v13 Simplification & Rules: Streamlined into a clear 6-chamber maze with subtle hints and one-way forward progression so entering a higher-numbered chamber never sends you back to a lower-numbered chamber.
