3-Level C++ OOP Adventure

ECLIPSE OF THE RELIC

A song of frost and ember echoes beneath the sunken labyrinth. Read the guide below, then begin your quest.

1. The Story

Beneath the ancient ruins lies a 9-chamber labyrinth caught between freezing winter tides and molten forge-fire-a quiet song of ice and fire woven into stone.

You can play as Kaelen (balanced scout), Lyra (swift relic seeker), or Thorne (tough iron warrior). Each chamber holds useful gear, helpful hints, or ancient guardians standing watch over the path ahead.

Across 3 levels, you must gather what you need, defeat the guardian of Chamber 9, and unlock the Final Gate before the shadows overtake the realm.

2. Your Objectives

  • Explore the 9 Chambers: Travel freely between lower and higher chambers using North, South, East, and West.
  • Collect Helpful Items: Pick up the Torch, Blade, Herb, Relic, and Key using Take to boost your strength, energy, or healing.
  • Defeat Chamber Guardians: Battle monsters like the Demon Monkey, Shadow Wraith, Obsidian Golem, and Astral Warden.
  • Open the Final Gate: Defeat the Astral Warden in Chamber 9 while holding the Key, then use Final Gate to advance through all 3 levels.

3. Rules & How to Play

Movement & Energy (STM): Moving costs 10 STM. If your STM hits 0, you lose 10 HP from exhaustion. Use Rest or a Torch to recover energy.
Health (HP) & Healing: If your HP reaches 0, the current level restarts. Use Use Herb (+45 HP) or Rest (+6 HP) when wounded.
Combat Choices: Use Slash for a steady hit, Vault (-25 STM) for heavy damage, Guard & Riposte to cut incoming damage in half and gain +20 STM, or Torch Flare (-20 STM) when carrying a Torch.
Restart & Audio: Use Restart Level or Restart Game anytime in the top bar. Toggle the Music or SFX & Voice buttons whenever you like.
